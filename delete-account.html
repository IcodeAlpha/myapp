Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

This page explains how users of Sipha (published by IcodeAlpha / Sipha) can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Sipha account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to support@sipha.app from the email address associated with your Sipha account (or include the email address / Google account linked to your profile).
  2. Use the subject line "Delete Sipha Account" and state that you want your account and associated data permanently deleted.
  3. We may ask for brief verification to confirm you own the account before processing the request.

Deletion requests are typically processed within 7 to 14 days of verification.

3. Data That Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, we delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Your account profile information and authentication linkage
  • Your personal book library, reading progress, and saved vocabulary words
  • Your reading circle memberships, reflections, quotes, and circle messages (or remove personal attribution where required)

4. Data That May Be Kept & Retention Periods

Certain limited information may be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, or system integrity:

  • Safety, moderation, and security logs: Records related to policy violations, abuse prevention, or security incidents may be retained for up to 90 days (or longer if required by law).
  • Encrypted backups: Residual copies in routine system backups are overwritten automatically within up to 30 days.
  • Legal obligations: Information required to comply with applicable laws or resolve disputes is kept only for as long as legally required.

5. Contact

If you have questions about account or data deletion for Sipha, contact support@sipha.app or privacy@sipha.app.
